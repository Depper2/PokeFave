 🐾 PokéFave Welcome to PokéFave – your ultimate Pokémon favorites manager! 🌟   
With PokéFave, you can **browse the first 151 Pokémon**, mark your favorites, edit them, see detailed stats, and even remove them if you change your mind. Think of it as your personal Pokédex… but way cooler!    🔥 Features  
- **Browse Pokémon**: Explore all 151 Pokémon with images and types.
- **Favorite Pokémon**: Mark any Pokémon as your favorite with a single click. 💖
- **Edit Favorites**: Update your favorite Pokémon notes or details. ✏️
- **View Details**: See full Pokémon stats, including height, weight, and types. 📊
- **Delete Favorites**: Remove Pokémon from your favorites list anytime. 🗑️
- **CRUD Functionality**: Full Create, Read, Update, Delete support for all your Pokémon favorites
